Germany Admissions & Visa FAQs

Everything you need to know about APS certification, blocked accounts, ECTS credits, and post-study visas.

Is the APS Certificate mandatory for Indian students?

Yes, since November 2022, the Academic Evaluation Centre (APS) certificate issued by the German Embassy in New Delhi is strictly mandatory for all Indian applicants before applying for a German student visa or enrolling in most German universities.

What is a Blocked Account and how much money is required?

A Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) is proof of financial resources for your stay in Germany. For 2026, you must deposit €11,904 (approx. ₹10.8 Lakhs), from which you can withdraw €992 per month for your daily living expenses once in Germany.

Do I need to learn German to study an English-taught Master's?

No, you can be admitted to 1,000+ English-taught Master's programs with just an IELTS/TOEFL score. However, learning conversational German (A1/A2 level) is highly recommended for daily social life, student jobs, and internships.

Can I work part-time while studying in Germany?

Yes, under updated German immigration regulations, international students can work up to 140 full days or 280 half days per year (equivalent to 20 hours/week). Working student (Werkstudent) roles often pay €13–€18/hour.

How does the 18-Month Job Seeker Visa work?

After graduating, you can extend your residence permit for up to 18 months to seek employment commensurate with your degree. Once you obtain a job offer with an appropriate salary, you can transition directly into an EU Blue Card or work permit.

UK Admissions & Visa FAQs

Everything you need to know about 1-year degrees, CAS requirements, IELTS waivers, and the 2-Year Graduate Route.

Is the 1-Year UK Master's degree valid in India and globally?

Yes, UK Master's degrees awarded by recognized universities are globally accredited and officially recognized in India by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) under the UK-India mutual recognition of educational qualifications agreement.

How can I get an IELTS waiver for UK universities?

Most UK partner universities grant an English language waiver if you scored 70% or higher in 12th standard English (CBSE, ICSE, or State Board) or if your undergraduate degree was taught entirely in English (Medium of Instruction letter provided).

How much maintenance funds do I need for my UK Student Visa?

UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) requires you to show unpaid tuition fees plus 9 months of living expenses: £1,483/month for Inner London (£13,348 total) or £1,136/month for Outside London (£10,224 total), held in a bank account for a continuous 28-day period.

Can I work part-time while studying in the UK?

Yes, international students holding a valid Student Route visa are legally permitted to work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time (40 hours/week) during official university vacation periods.

How does the 2-Year Graduate Route (PSW) work?

Upon successfully passing your 1-year master's course, you can apply from within the UK for the Graduate Route Visa. It grants 2 years of unrestricted work authorization without needing an employer sponsor or minimum salary threshold.

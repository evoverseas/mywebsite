Why Study in Australia?

A Premier Destination for Global Students

Post-Study Work Visa (PSWR)

International graduates can apply for a Temporary Graduate Visa, allowing them to work in Australia for 2 to 4 years depending on their qualification and regional location.

Quality of Life

Australian cities like Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane consistently rank among the top most livable cities in the world, offering a safe, multicultural environment with a great climate.

High Part-Time Wages

Australia has one of the highest minimum wages in the world. International students can comfortably cover their living expenses while working part-time (up to 48 hours per fortnight).

Top Australian Partner Universities

We partner with leading institutions across all major Australian states and regional areas.

Estimated Costs & Requirements

  • Tuition: AUD 25,000 - 45,000 / year
  • Living: AUD 24,000 / year (Required for Visa)
  • Exams: IELTS/PTE (Mandatory)
  • Work: 48 hours per fortnight

Your Journey to Australia

A transparent, step-by-step process

1. Course & University Selection (Month 1)

We match your academic profile and career goals with CRICOS-registered courses and universities across Australia.

2. Offer & GTE Clearance (Month 2-3)

After receiving an offer, we guide you through the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) or Genuine Student (GS) requirement, ensuring your financials and statements meet strict Australian standards.

3. CoE & Visa (Month 4-5)

Once you pay the initial deposit and receive your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), we help arrange your OSHC (health cover) and file your Subclass 500 Student Visa application.

Australia Admissions & Visa FAQs

Everything you need to know about the Genuine Student (GS) test, financial thresholds, and Post-Study Work rights.

What is the new Genuine Student (GS) requirement for Australian Visas?

The Australian Government replaced the old GTE statement with the Genuine Student (GS) assessment. Applicants must answer specific targeted questions demonstrating their genuine intent to study, past academic trajectory, and how the course delivers significant economic value upon returning to India.

How much living funds must I show for the Australian Subclass 500 Visa?

As per the latest Department of Home Affairs (DHA) update, international students must demonstrate evidence of financial capacity of at least AUD $29,710/year (approx. ₹16.5 Lakhs) for living expenses, in addition to 1 year of tuition fees.

What are the part-time work limits in Australia for students?

International students holding a valid Subclass 500 visa can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during academic terms and unlimited hours during recognized course vacations and semester breaks.

What is the duration of the Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)?

Graduates from master's degree programs receive 2 years of Post-Study Work Rights in major cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane), and up to 3 to 4 years if studying in designated regional university locations (like Adelaide, Perth, Darwin, or Gold Coast).

Which English language tests are accepted for Australia?

The Department of Home Affairs accepts IELTS Academic (min 6.0–6.5) and PTE Academic (min 58–64 score). EV Overseas provides end-to-end test preparation coaching to hit top score bands.

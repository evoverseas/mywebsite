Why Study in the USA?

The Land of Innovation and Opportunity

STEM OPT Extension

International students in STEM fields can apply for a 24-month extension of their Optional Practical Training (OPT), allowing for up to 3 years of work experience in the US after graduation.

World-Class Education

The US is home to the majority of the world's top-ranked universities, offering cutting-edge research facilities, flexible curriculums, and unparalleled academic prestige.

Incredible Career ROI

With major tech hubs like Silicon Valley, Seattle, and Austin, studying in the US provides direct access to internships and jobs at the world's largest companies.

USA Admissions & Visa FAQs

Everything you need to know about STEM OPT, backlogs, GRE waivers, and F-1 appointments.

Is GRE mandatory for MS programs in the USA for Fall 2026?

No, over 70% of leading US universities now offer GRE waivers or test-optional policies for STEM MS programs (e.g., MS in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity). However, submitting a 310+ GRE score can significantly strengthen scholarship chances for competitive applicants.

How many backlogs are accepted by US universities?

Most reputable US public and private universities accept up to 10–15 backlogs if your overall undergraduate percentage is 60%+ (or CGPA 6.2+) and you demonstrate strong technical projects, research, or relevant work experience.

How does the 3-Year STEM OPT extension work?

International graduates with an F-1 visa who complete a qualifying STEM degree can work in the United States for up to 36 months (12 months standard OPT + 24 months STEM extension) without requiring an immediate H-1B work visa.

When should I start my application process for Fall 2026?

US university priority deadlines open in September 2025 and run through January 2026. Applying early ensures maximum eligibility for university department scholarships, Graduate Assistantships (GA/TA), and preferred F-1 visa interview slot availability at the Hyderabad consulate.
